Model, assumptions & limits

Equations
  • Sun: declination δ=23.45°·sin(360/365·(284+n)); solar time = clock + (4·(lon−15·tz)+EoT)/60; sun vector (E,N,Up) from latitude, δ, hour angle ω.
  • Panel normal n=(sinβ sinγ, sinβ cosγ, cosβ); tilt β, azimuth γ (0°=N, 90°=E, 180°=S, 270°=W). cosθ = sun·n.
  • Gpoa = DNI·max(0,cosθ) + DHI·(1+cosβ)/2 + ρ·GHI·(1−cosβ)/2, ρ=0.2 (isotropic sky model).
  • P = min(Prated, Prated·Gpoa/1000·(1−shade)·(1−0.004·(Tcell−25))), Tcell=Tamb+0.03125·G (NOCT 45 °C). Area = Prated/(1000·η).
  • Energy = Σ P·Δt (5-min steps). Improvement % = 100·(Eopt−Efixed)/Efixed.
Optimizer & ML
  • Scheduled: tilt/azimuth candidates (10° / 15° grid within your limits) are scored per interval, then a dynamic program picks the sequence maximizing total energy minus movement cost. The cos θ objective is non-convex, so a grid search is safer than gradient methods, and the DP is exact for the discretized problem. If moves exceed your daily limit, the movement penalty is raised until it complies.
  • ML: 20-tree Random Forest regressor trained on synthetic labels from the physical model (random days, times, orientations); features: sun elevation, azimuth, tilt, panel azimuth, DNI, DHI. It only approximates the physics. Select “ML surrogate” to let the optimizer use it; all reported energies are still evaluated with the physical model.
Limitations & research ideas
  • Clear-sky model, no cloud dynamics, horizon shading, soiling, or inverter losses; no real data.
  • Tracking is an upper bound; small installations rarely justify motors and maintenance.
  • Hypotheses to test (not established inventions): move only if expected gain > actuator cost; forecast uncertainty; online learning from measured output. No patentability is claimed without a prior-art search.
Low-cost prototype idea
  • 5–10 W panel, two hobby servos (tilt + azimuth), ESP32, INA219 current sensor, small reference cell or LDR; log measured power per orientation and compare with this model.
